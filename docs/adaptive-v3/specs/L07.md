The isolated proposal contains **104 pose nodes, 284 directed edges, 16 junctions
and 44 capacity-one reservation groups**. It uses the accepted L05 geometry:
forklift 1.20×2.10×3.00 m, LONG 4.20×0.52×0.65 m transverse across the forks,
unchanged cradle, 0.25 m horizontal/top padding and rear-steer radius 0.808290377 m.
C08 payload/support/attachment approval remains required. No backend operations,
3D geometry or old contract is changed.

The loaded network follows X6/72/138 and Z38/67 on the 8 m cross/spine, 10 m north
band and 12 m perimeters. Four gateway poses face south, at X22/52/92/122 and
Z35.80 for HEAVY_W/LONG_W/LONG_E/HEAVY_E. The padded fork tip remains 0.05 m north
of storage boundary Z34. These nodes prove clear-floor gateway access, not access
to unplaced internal slots; L09 and C08 must add pickup/setdown approaches.

All six accepted port service positions and facing directions are retained.
IN_W admits its six forklift cargo families with F01/FL01; IN_N forklift service
admits PALLET_LOAD only. OUT_W admits its four forklift families with F02/FL02;
OUT_E_B2B admits its six. Manual-only IN_E_PARCEL and OUT_S_EXPRESS gain **bare,
unloaded geometric vehicle access**, without adding forklift cargo service.
Transiting a semantic service pose does not start a port operation. The query
must separately check its `service_action` cargo/pair permission.

Three exact profiles distinguish bare vehicle, vehicle plus empty cradle, and
the full loaded component envelope. The 4 m south branch is bare-only, with no
cargo or attached cradle. It was checked with the complete unchanged bare vehicle;
this is not a reduced proxy for LONG. Other named loads require C08 to prove
their actual unit plus support lies inside the selected conservative profile.
No dimensions or masses are changed to obtain a route.

Each node stores floor `world_pos` for the vehicle root, `yaw_rad` and
`front_axle_world_pos`. Local +Z is forward; positive yaw about +Y faces +X from +Z.
Node yaw wraps to [0,2π); each edge has an explicit continuous unwrapped yaw.
Each directed edge states motion sign, clear width/height, profile/cargo/pair
permissions, reservation groups and both front-axle/root path lengths.

The nonsteered front axle is 0.40 m ahead of the root. Evaluate the analytic edge:

| Primitive | Front axle F and heading θ |
|---|---|
| Straight | `F(u)=lerp(F0,F1,u)`; θ constant |
| Rear-steer arc | `θ(u)=θ0+u(θ1−θ0)`; `F=[Ix−q cosθ, Iz+q sinθ]`, q=±0.808290377 m |
| Root, for either primitive | `root=F−0.40[sinθ,cosθ]` |

Arcs are real 90° moving turns. Virtual rear steer is −60° for positive forward
yaw curvature and +60° for the opposite curvature. Reverse edges traverse the
same curve backwards with preserved physical facing and steering. Curvature or
motion-direction changes require a stop/steering transition; this proposal does
not assign durations. Short reversing shunts reach the exact accepted W/E service
poses. No edge performs a stationary yaw change.

On these arcs the root-path tangent differs from chassis yaw by **26.3295°**.
Using a generic `lookAt(nextRoot)` would be wrong, and reverse travel must not
flip yaw by π. The stored five-degree pose samples are witnesses with explicit
root/front-axle/yaw columns. They are **not** instructions to interpolate chords:
runtime consumers must evaluate the analytic primitive and preserve yaw.

L05's centred full-circle square was a sufficient local bound. Actual fillets
have offset ICRs and cover only a quarter turn; their complete components are
checked against the actual union of reserved clear bands. Storage, reserve,
staging and packing rectangles are treated as solid obstacles. No width or
obstacle is relaxed to make an arc fit.

Straight edges use exact swept component rectangles. On arcs, every padded
oriented component is checked at ≤0.5° yaw steps. For a maximum component-corner
radius ρ and yaw step Δθ, additional inflation
`ε=2ρ sin(Δθ/4)` covers displacement to the nearest sample throughout the interval.
SAT collision checks use padding+ε, so the proof covers continuous motion between
samples. Reverse geometry is identical and reused, while its endpoints, yaw and
steering are independently checked. The current run checked **67,805 component
poses**, with no collision against any non-clear zone or wall; all heights fit.

Eighteen route examples include both W/E LONG and HEAVY gateway approaches,
north pallet receiving, west retail pallet shipping and all six bare-vehicle
service poses from a common central pose. Inbound and outbound jobs use their
separate fixed pairs rather than switching vehicles within one route.

| Conditional route, root-path metres | West gateway | East gateway |
|---|---:|---:|
| IN_W → LONG, inbound pair | 70.017 | 110.017 |
| LONG → OUT_E, outbound pair | 110.017 | 70.017 |
| IN_W → HEAVY, pallet candidate | 40.017 | 140.017 |
| HEAVY → OUT_E, pallet candidate | 140.017 | 40.017 |

These are geometric root-path lengths, including reverse legs, not job times,
labour or throughput. Source scheduling must account for handling, steering,
traffic, waits and the actual travel model. No buffer-cell pickup path is claimed.

Negative checks reject loaded/attached-cradle access to the bare south branch,
an actual forced LONG footprint there (it collides with packing), an injected
obstacle on a real turn, forbidden LONG at IN_N, wrong/missing pairs, manual-only
forklift service and the root-tangent yaw recipe. The latter misplaces the front
axle, despite a superficially smooth root curve.

The resource policy remains 24 people and two existing F01/FL01 / F02/FL02 pairs.
Opposite, reverse and intersecting movements share capacity-one reservations;
this is no per-edge vehicle pool. A real port action also reserves its accepted
worker/service resources. Pedestrian conflict arbitration and actual operational
timelines remain source integration requirements. The SVG was actually opened
through CPU-only Sharp; an overflowing east-port subtitle was wrapped and the
final figure reopened. **VERIFY**, pending root graph/plan review; L09 not started.
