The forklift is **1.20×2.10×3.00 m** in X/Y/Z. LONG remains **4.20×0.52×0.65 m**,
upright and transverse across the forks. There is no relative load yaw or scale
change. Fork heels are at Z0.40, tips at Z1.50 and travel top Y0.30; fork centres
X±0.40 match the cradle pockets. The 3.20×0.15×0.85 m cradle contacts the load at
Y0.45, with support-pad centres X±1.40. Nominal longitudinal load centre is
Z1.00−Z0.40 = **0.60 m** ahead of the fork heel. End distance from each pad centre
is 0.70 m, and frame-end overhang is 0.50 m. These are geometric assumptions:
C08 must approve support span/overhang, actual gross bundle+cradle mass/centre of
gravity, rated payload at that centre/height, lock/restraint and pickup support.
No payload rating is invented; the L04 15 kg manual limit is unrelated to forklift
capacity.

Horizontal clearance padding is 0.25 m per side; top padding is 0.25 m. Straight
requirements are therefore `4.20+2×0.25 = 4.70 m` width and
`max(2.10,0.97)+0.25 = 2.35 m` height. W has 2.30 m extra width after padding; E has
1.30 m; both have 2.15 m extra height. Cross the aperture straight and aligned;
turn only after the whole loaded profile is inside, or before outbound crossing.

The rotation bound includes rear steering. Front axle Z0.40 and rear axle Z−1.00
give a 1.40 m wheelbase. A synthetic virtual rear-axle-centre steer of 60° places
the instantaneous centre of rotation at local X/Z:

`ICR = [1.40/tan(60°), 0.40] = [0.808290377, 0.40] m`.

This is a moving rear-steer vehicle, rather than an in-place spin about its root.
With 0.70 m track, Ackermann rear-wheel angles are 71.874° inner / 50.397° outer,
inside the proposed 75° individual limit. Rear tyre radius 0.225 m / width 0.20 m
has maximum lateral extent `0.35+sqrt(0.225²+0.10²)=0.596221 m`, within the 0.60 m
half width. Actual C08/M14.1 geometry and steering must retain these operating
bounds or trigger a fresh L05 calculation.

The farthest corner of the *union bounding rectangle* from that ICR gives:

`r = sqrt((2.10+0.808290377)² + (1.50+0.40)²) = 3.473924714 m`.

After padding, `r_pad=3.723924714 m` and full swept diameter is **7.447849427 m**.
The rectangle includes empty corners, so this overbounds the actual components.
A full circle covers continuous yaw; sampled component/corner rotations provide
an independent numerical cross-check.

The 8 m spine X68–76 and cross Z34–42 provide an 8×8 m intersection. With the
**ICR**, not the vehicle root, at world [72,0,38], the padded circle fits inside
X68.276075…75.723925 / Z34.276075…41.723925. Total spare side length is 0.552151 m.
The sufficient admissible ICR interval is X71.723925…72.276075 and
Z37.723925…38.276075. L07 must still construct continuous entry/exit trajectories
and check actual obstacles, shared resources and transient traffic.

At the two specified service poses, the transformed ICR is [6.40,0,17.191710] or
[138.40,0,17.191710]. Full padded turn circles occupy X2.676075…10.123925 or
X134.676075…142.123925, with Z13.467785…20.915634: both lie within their 12×12 m
approach squares. This local result does not establish a full buffer-to-gate path.
Opposite steering is also contained: its ICR Z is 18.808290, with padded Z bounds
15.084366…22.532215 and the same X bounds.
OUT_E_B2B stages LONG along world X, requiring a loaded-vehicle 90° reorientation
before gate crossing. Its 2 m-deep buffer cells are not turn bays. Four-metre
local links and the south-transfer lane along X reject this transverse LONG
profile because its padded width is 4.70 m.

Admission checks port cargo permission first, then exact dimensions/scale and
orientation, correct exclusive handler, approved C08 support/rating, and physical
gate/path bounds. IN_N_STANDARD is physically 6×4.5 m and would fit the geometric
projection, but **forbids LONG**. It returns proposed `PORT_CARGO_FORBIDDEN`; a
wide gate cannot override the matrix. No shared reason-code registry is changed.

missing cradle/rating approval, forbidden IN_N cargo, a 7 m turn cell, a 4 m lane
