# L01 — V3 physical envelope proposal

Isolated proposal: [layout-v3.envelope.json](../../v3/contract-proposals/layout-v3.envelope.json).
It defines the outer coordinate envelope only, not an accepted engine layout or
usable storage capacity. V2/shared contracts remain unchanged.

| Quantity | V3 proposal | V2 baseline | Exact ratio |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| X span | 144 m | 72 m | 2 |
| Z span | 72 m | 48 m | 1.5 |
| Y height | 8 m | 8 m | 1 |
| Floor area X×Z | 10,368 m² | 3,456 m² | 3 |
| Gross envelope volume X×Z×Y | 82,944 m³ | 27,648 m³ | 3 |

Coordinates are right-handed `[X,Y,Z]`, Y up, metres and seconds. Origin is the
minimum floor corner. Bounds are `[0,0,0]..[144,8,72]`: X=0…144, Z=0…72, Y=0…8.
The JSON's bounds vectors have exactly three numbers; `extent_m` names each axis
to avoid confusing the 72 m depth with the 8 m height.

Area: `144×72=10,368`; volume: `10,368×8=82,944`.
Baseline from read-only `contracts/layout.example.json`: `72×48=3,456` and
`3,456×8=27,648`. Both area and gross-volume ratios equal exactly 3. The independent
check also computes XZ polygon area by shoelace and volume from XYZ spans; exact
integer/Fraction results are recorded in [L01 evidence](../evidence/L01.json).

World scale remains **1 world unit = 1 metre**. People, carts, forklifts and cargo
do not grow or shrink with the warehouse; a nominal person remains about 1.70 m.
New archetype dimensions will be explicit metadata. This is a newly configured
envelope, not a globally scaled copy of the V2 master.

Sources: V3 brief `02_DESIGN_V3.md` §2 and the actually viewed, accepted R07 version 2
PNG/provenance. R07's 8 m building and 4.5 m clear large-gate illustration are
consistent concept context. L01 does not certify door clearance, loaded-vehicle
manoeuvres or usable internal height. Gross volume is not usable slot volume.

Zones, ports, graphs, slots, capacities and 3D geometry remain undefined here.
They belong to later cards; L02 has not been started by this executor. Status is
VERIFY until root reads the JSON, calculations and numerical evidence.
