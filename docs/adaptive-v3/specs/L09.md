HEAVY's useful height is free headroom above an open platform. Its operating
envelope is 1.60×1.80×1.40 m; no fictitious enclosed cavity inside the .18 m base
is asserted. All slots admit one handling unit, without automatic stacking.
Outer geometry, operating bounds and usable volume remain distinct.
Declared structural post bounds also stay outside useful cavities: SMALL has four
0.10 m posts, STANDARD six 0.07 m posts, LONG three 0.16 m rear posts, and FLEX two
0.10 m rear posts with an open front. Deck tops match shelf Y; HEAVY specifies
three runners, nine support blocks and two .10×.18 m clear fork pockets. Geometry
targets do not certify their load rating or replace C08 support checks.

SMALL is low manual picking. STANDARD has two upper reserve-only levels until
an approved lift process exists; its ground level supports manual loads ≤15 kg
or conditional forklift service. HEAVY and LONG require the approved handling
method. Upper LONG is reserve-only pending support/lift approval. FLEX is finite,
manual-only small/standard storage up to 15 kg. A manual service ceiling of 1.25 m
is synthetic; it does not permit floor workers to reach upper addresses.

The generator preserves L06's .35 m pedestrian / .95 m combined cart+operator
envelopes and L05/L07's complete vehicle/LONG/cradle geometry. Cargo mass, packing
and final eligibility remain C02/C08. No mesh or backend operation is produced.

IDs derive from region, module ordinal, level and bay. The count comes from real
module/level/bay products; 864 is not a target. All 188 reserve-only addresses set
`retrieval_blocked=true` and reject retrieval pending an actual approved handling
process. They are never inaccessible active inventory. The 24 HEAVY positions are
shared by four eligible families; the 64 ground STANDARD addresses are shared by
standard/fragile candidates. C02's complete 200-SKU mapping and actual stock must
respect these shared capacities, actual mass and permitted handling. Counts per
archetype cannot be summed as if every family owned its own address pool.

SMALL uses three 3 m north/south cart aisles and a clear west/east entrance at
Z49, matching the existing L06 gateway on each perimeter. Each aisle has two
facing rows, four modules per row, five bins and two low levels. STANDARD uses two
eight-module rows, a 6.8 m central north/south aisle and 6.9 m between rows. Its
front services connect to L06 gateway X52/92, Z40.75. The separate RESERVE rows are
reached through the same central opening and the clear north cross band.

HEAVY reserves a 6 m central aisle and a wide pedestrian/cart cross at Z25.
Twelve ground positions per side remain sparse enough for actual forklift turns.
LONG has six wide modules per bank, with a broad empty apron and two real
rear-steer turns to each pre-handle stop. FLEX has four finite manual bays per
region, connected from its southern L06 gateway. No module occupies a protected
peripheral, cross or spine band.

Worker terminals sit 0.36 m outside the open module face; the .35 m body envelope
therefore remains outside the fixture. Usable faces are 0.05 m inside the outer
front, with front-aligned cargo clearance .03/.05 m. Horizontal reach is thus
**.44–.46 m**, within the root-provided P01/P08 constraint **.4712 m**. Active manual
grasp heights stay at or below 1.25 m, and gross manual mass must be ≤15 kg.
Specific posture, actual hand/box contact and loaded transfer to the stopped cart
remain C/P-series checks; the numeric floor access does not certify an animation.

Vehicle approaches start at exact L07 gateway poses or an explicitly referenced
fraction of an existing straight edge. They evaluate analytic front-axle lines
and rear-steer arcs with separately stored yaw/root position. Reverse exit follows
the same curves backwards without a spin. LONG uses the unchanged L05 vehicle,
cradle and 4.20 m bundle bounds. Nonlong ground travel uses the unchanged forklift
plus actual enclosed heavy/fragile size bounds; the wide LONG cradle must be
absent. This is a distinct approved-state requirement, not an implicit detach or
shrinking of a load.

All vehicle stops keep the padded fork tip 0.05 m outside the fixture. These are
pre-handle floor poses. C08 must still prove fork insertion, support, payload,
attachment, vertical lift and the exact stored-to-carried transformation. Upper
STANDARD/LONG service-floor reach does not approve retrieval. In particular,
STANDARD_CARTON forklift transport is not silently added to L07; upper reserve
retrieval stays blocked until an accepted handling process exists.

The generator validates module bounds/overlaps, usable 3D bounds/volume and
aggregate storage payload, target cargo fit and unique IDs. Human/cart links use
complete swept capsules, not point lines. Vehicle lines use exact component
sweeps; arcs check oriented components at ≤0.5° with analytic displacement
inflation between samples. Other occupied regions, every fixture and walls stay
obstacles. Negative cases include oversized cargo, overweight units, upper manual
retrieval, manual LONG, stacking a second HU, forbidden tilt and reserve retrieval.
