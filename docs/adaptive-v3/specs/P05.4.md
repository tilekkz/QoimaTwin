# P05.4 Receiver elbow and native hand clearance

The reported reversed elbow is reproduced at receive t=8 on the actual R01 rig: the inherited near-minus-pi elbow swivel puts the elbow 83.182mm above the shoulder-to-wrist chord with positive native signed bend, despite terminal contact error of only 28.5nm. The first low-elbow correction is retained as a second negative control: it reaches the same point but intersects the hand's original C-mouth volume. Neither contact accuracy nor Euler limits alone establish a valid pose.

The correction keeps the measured lower-pad contact point and opposed native pad/screen face normals. It rotates the hand 30 degrees about that contact normal; the original full hand quaternion was an inherited P04 constraint, not a physical requirement. The selected arm branch uses shoulder yaw -0.2rad, elbow swivel 1.5rad and palm roll -5pi/16. Three native-rig solutions define contact and the 1cm/2cm free approach. The actual pad and screen triangles, distances and normals are recorded by the checker. No contact point, geometry, scale or numeric joint limit changes.

The same 2.4-second preparation first abducts the straight arm to upper-arm Z=0.7rad, folds outside the body at upper-arm X=-0.5rad/forearm X=-1rad, then moves the coordinated chain to its native approach solution. The two intermediate poses occur at fractions 0.25 and 0.55; quintic interpolation reverses them for return. The builder derives untouched joint values from the native rig. Total receive duration remains 17.8s, scan duration 2.5s, and the original contact window, body placement, HU, feet and inventory remain unchanged.

`node frontend/tools/build-receiver-timeline-v3.mjs --check-only` recomputes the 14 keys and intermediate poses and compares exact timeline bytes. The historical native contact report supplies only the measured pad coordinate; its old arm acceptance is not promoted or rehashed. The solver prints bounded endpoint evidence and writes no historical reports.

`node frontend/tools/check-receiver-elbows-v3.mjs` uses the real worker factory, controller and GLBs. It requires the original branch to fail the new elbow gate and the first low-elbow branch to fail the exact native hand-mouth guard. It checks all four modes at 10ms, stage/key boundaries, 100 arbitrary seeks, three rigid cell transforms, original limits, actor/HU identity, fixed HU transform, invalid inputs and disposal/rebinding. The existing runtime gate also runs with its output/cache redirected into an isolated temporary directory. `--check-only` fully recomputes the owned evidence and compares its exact bytes.

For a wrist more than 150mm forward and no more than 150mm above its shoulder, reject elbow pole Y above 20mm or positive signed bend above 0.15rad about the pre-swivel upper-arm X axis. This is a targeted front low-reach regression, not a universal anatomy certificate. Every dense frame also checks actual fore-sleeve/cuff triangles against the original hand-local C-mouth box, shrunk by 2micrometres, plus whole-mesh containment of its centre. Contact frames require the same measured point within 2micrometres and opposed native face normals.

Bounded native clearance compares 37 actual poses per version, including preparation/return samples, with all six arm meshes against the fixed workcell and all other body meshes. Proximal upper arm versus head/ears/hair/helmet additionally runs at all 482 preparation/return poses per version. Connected components prune bounds only: positive witnesses require winding and surface depth against each entire original mesh, with up to 16 inward triangle-centroid probes per component/direction. Synthetic overlap and boundary controls must pass.

Exact upper-sleeve versus adjacent shoulder-band/shirt/vest mesh observations are reported separately, including witness points, matrices and depths for old and corrected paths. They are proximal model-junction observations, not a proof of clearance; head, ears, neck, legs, distal-arm/torso and workcell collisions are never waived. Nonadjacent body/workcell witnesses or dense head/mouth failures block this gate. Internal right-arm pairs are outside the penetration scan but the hand mouth is checked explicitly. No sampled witness is not a complete or continuous collision certificate. Full native Blender checks and browser acceptance remain separate; this task uses CPU only.
