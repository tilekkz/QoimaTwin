The plan partitions the accepted 144×72 m floor into 40 disjoint rectangles.
Coordinates are metres, X east/right and Z north/up on this diagram; Y remains
the vertical axis. The 8 m building envelope and metric scale are unchanged.
All new boundaries are synthetic design assumptions. This is a proposal, not a
replacement for shared contracts or an operational layout accepted by the engine.

| Class | West bounds X / Z (m) | East bounds X / Z (m) | Area per bank | X gap between areas |
|---|---|---|---:|---:|
| SMALL | 12–32 / 42–56 | 112–132 / 42–56 | 280 m² | 80 m |
| STANDARD | 36–68 / 42–54 | 76–108 / 42–54 | 384 m² | 8 m |
| HEAVY | 12–32 / 16–34 | 112–132 / 16–34 | 360 m² | 80 m |
| LONG | 36–68 / 16–34 | 76–108 / 16–34 | 576 m² | 8 m |
| FLEX | 36–48 / 12–16 | 96–108 / 12–16 | 48 m² | 48 m |

Each class has two separated candidate areas, preserving west/east choice for
later port-dependent costs. SMALL is for low bins; STANDARD for ordinary cartons;
HEAVY for ground/reinforced pallet handling; LONG reserves wide support/manoeuvre
space. FLEX admits only small/standard candidates, has two finite footprints and
cannot occupy a corridor. These are preliminary family permissions: dimensions,
orientation, payload, access height, equipment and loaded route checks still apply
to each future slot. Two separate 56 m² RESERVE footprints are not active pick stock.

Protected circulation consists of 12 m west/east perimeter bands, a 10 m north
band, the 8 m central spine X68–76, an 8 m cross band Z34–42, a 4 m south transfer
band Z8–12 and local links at least 4 m wide. Staging, racks, FLEX and conveyor
supports are excluded from these rectangles. A geometric connectivity check
covers this clear floor; it does not certify traffic capacity or swept turns.
The 8 m widths are synthetic provisional assumptions pending L05/L07/C08 loaded
feasibility, rather than a safety certification.

These overlays annotate existing clear floor and add no area. Finite inbound and
outbound staging rectangles sit beside approaches, rather than inside them.
Northern receiving has two small staging pads. The southern express marshal is
separate from its throat. The south-east 624 m² workspace reserves six packing
stations and a light conveyor; no station positions or conveyor topology are set.
The original 24 people remain context, without new assignments or scale changes.

Root's actual first-draft plan review found the 16 m spine / 14 m cross too sparse.
The revised 8 m / 8 m bands release floor into both storage banks. Peripheral port
approaches, southern passage and the 624 m² packing reservation remain unchanged.

Storage gains **928 m²** (39.2%); circulation releases 864 m², with the remaining
64 m² from compacting reserve/staging. These gross footprints are not usable
storage volume, slot counts or utilization. Later rack/slot proposals must
demonstrate capacity and the 200-SKU assortment; this diagram makes no such
acceptance claim.

R02 supplies distinct storage purposes. R07 v2 and R08 v1 were actually viewed:
their six boundary locations, heterogeneous banks and south packing concept are
retained. Exact reference-art rectangles are replaced by west/east class pairs,
separate staging and an explicit clear cross/spine system. This prevents the
illustrative FLEX/packing masses from silently consuming access. Reference-art
gate widths do not establish engineering clearance here.

Validation uses exact rectangle intersection/area, an independent 1 m occupancy
map, clear-floor flood fill, approach coverage, paired separation and SVG/JSON
coordinate agreement. The actual SVG was rasterized with CPU-only Sharp and
opened: all ten class areas, six port markers, staging labels, scale and reserved
bands are visible without clipping. No route graph, racks, capacities, port service
anchors, geometry or L03 work is included. **VERIFY**, pending root plan review.
