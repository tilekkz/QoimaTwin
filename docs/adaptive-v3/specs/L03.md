Three inbound ports retain the brief/L02 coordinates. Positions below are floor
references `[X,Y,Z]` in metres. Service IDs are `v3:service:<PORT_ID>`; these are
semantic references for later graph construction, not an authored route graph.

| Port | Boundary position | Inward normal | Service position | Provisional clear W×H | Buffer ceiling |
|---|---|---|---|---|---:|
| IN_W_BULK | [0,0,18] | [1,0,0] | [6,0,18] | 7×4.5 m | 2 HU |
| IN_N_STANDARD | [72,0,72] | [0,0,-1] | [72,0,66] | 6×4.5 m | 4 HU |
| IN_E_PARCEL | [144,0,54] | [-1,0,0] | [138,0,54] | 4×3.5 m | 8 HU |

Each service point lies strictly inside its protected approach on reserved clear
floor. Buffer cargo belongs in the separate L02 staging zones: W bulk has two
positions, north has two in each of its west/east pads, and east parcel has eight.
These are finite count ceilings, subject to actual footprint, orientation and
payload admission; they do not assert that every allowed load will fit. Intake
reserves admissible staging first. A full/incompatible buffer holds work upstream,
without parking cargo on the approach or teleporting it to a slot.

The chosen family and deterministic intake-time matrix is explicit:

| Port | Allowed archetypes and synthetic seconds per HU |
|---|---|
| W | HEAVY_BOX 40; PALLET_LOAD 30; IBC 40; LONG_BUNDLE 55; DRUM 35; FRAGILE_CRATE 45 |
| N | STANDARD_CARTON 18; PALLET_LOAD 30 |
| E | SMALL_CARTON 10; STANDARD_CARTON 14 |

One job handles one handling unit, not one SKU unit or lot. Times cover intake,
unload/check and release to staging. They exclude worker/vehicle travel, resource
waiting, putaway and other legs. An open window is `[0,28800)` elapsed run seconds,
intersected with the supplied horizon. These synthetic assumptions generate no
arrivals, inventory, throughput or engine KPI.

Every port has a one-job bay limit and references **RECEIVERS_SHARED**, choosing
one of existing R01/R02. W also requires **INBOUND_FORKLIFT_PAIR** for all its
allowed families; N requires that pair for PALLET_LOAD and manual receiving for
STANDARD_CARTON. E uses manual receiving only, within future worker/load limits.
The pair resolves once in the root-owned policy to F01 + FL01. Actual actor and
vehicle IDs are reserved exclusively across all ports, with relocation in the
source timeline. Three bay limits provide neither three receivers nor three
drivers. The exact existing 24-person roster is retained; F02/FL02 remains the
separate outbound pair. No resource pool is copied into this proposal.

Gate dimensions are provisional candidates. Their openings lie within the
reserved approach spans and under the 8 m envelope, but allowed family membership
does not prove loaded clearance, payload, stability or swept turns. L05 and later
cargo/route gates must accept the actual dimensions and handling method first.
R07 v2 was actually viewed; its large-gate 4.5 m height is concept context only.

Validation checks the three IDs, exact positions/normals, inward service offset,
clear-floor containment and staging boundary connectivity, gate opening bounds,
matrix/time coverage, finite buffer allocations and shared-resource references
against the actual current actor contract. There is no geometry, route graph,
outbound-port implementation or change to old contracts. **VERIFY** pending root
review of the JSON and evidence.
