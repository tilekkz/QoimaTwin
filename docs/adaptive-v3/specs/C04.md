There are 588 active **geometric candidates**. Separate RESERVE_W/E regions contain eight STANDARD modules/48 blocked slots; the other 140 blocked slots are upper levels in primary regions. RESERVE is a region/access classification, never a sixth storage family. Retrieval remains blocked with the exact source reason; reserve records have no currently offered handler alternative even when their floor graph references exist.

Every native L09 slot and module field is retained unchanged, including IDs, zone, bay/level, module yaw, usable local XYZ dimensions, rotated world bounds, bottom-centre anchor, payload/HU limits, source handler strings and worker/cart/vehicle references. Added local bounds/anchors are verified independently against bay/level geometry and quarter-turn world transforms. All 3,680 access references resolve to actual L12 walk/vehicle nodes or source-path maps and the appropriate semantic local-service/path definitions.

Physical module outer/operating bounds, usable cargo bounds and module payload aggregation remain separate. `module_physical_bounds_world_broadphase` is a collision broadphase AABB, not usable space or a solid enclosed shelf cavity. HEAVY has a physical .18m-high base and 1.50m usable open headroom above its .18m support plane; that air is not a tall platform mesh. Module limits remain SMALL150, STANDARD1500, HEAVY1000, LONG1200 and FLEX15kg, with source finite HU capacities. The sum of complete HU gross masses across all module slots must respect the module limit; no actual mass or stock is assigned here.

Placement retains the existing `HU dimension + 2c <= usable dimension` budget on every local axis. X is centred with at least c per face; Z is front-aligned with c at the front and at least c behind. HU bottom rests directly on `bin_anchor.Y`, bottom gap **0**. The entire vertical **2c** budget is headroom above the HU. `service_height_m` is the cargo support plane, not an actual grip target. For a synthetic FRAGILE dimension query at STANDARD ground, supportY.20 plus HUheight.95 gives topY1.15; usable ceiling1.25 leaves exactly .10m above and zero below. This does not grant another clearance allowance or a human/handling pose.

C01's `upright_xyz_in_module → upright_xyz` mapping is identity `[+X,+Y,+Z]`; the module's world yaw applies separately. Tipping, reflecting or yaw90 relative to the module receives no permission. Exact SHA bindings include accepted C01 schema/example, L09, L12 private manifest, semantic index, compiled links, public runtime and the C01 R03 target. Changed source bytes require an explicit future update rather than silent rebinding.

Handler alternatives are conditional capability requirements, with `availability_asserted=false` and `operational_admission=false`. There are 552 manual-carry and 552 cart alternatives limited to 15kg, plus 100 forklift alternatives. These are overlapping options, not new people/resources. Ground STANDARD forklift alternatives allow **FRAGILE_CRATE only**, preserving the source rule; STANDARD_CARTON is not silently added. HEAVY/LONG require forklift/operator and approved support, with actual C08 support, mass/CoG, rating, lift and attachment checks still pending. No upper/reserve retrieval is unlocked.
