# P03.5.3.1 — compact manual reach gripper reference

Actual square raster `assets/references/v3/p03-small-gripper.png` was generated with built-in image_gen and opened before tool geometry. First candidate was rejected for left/right jaw orientation; retained V2 corrects to front/backZ sliders and four fingers in two depth rows. Its front view shows two leading fingers; its side view shows opposing front/back fingers. U frame, rear rubber Tbar, silver arms and restrained petrol/orange mechanism fit the existing industrial cartoon direction.

Exact pre-geometry engineering targets are in `p03-small-gripper.targets.json`:200mm handle,230mm centre reach,40/54mm jaw gap, four20×36×12mm pads, two existing60×40×40mm/35g primary packages. Overall200×168×310mm includes captured front guide ends; this explicitly clarifies the approximate art-card total depth. Tool mass650g and spring linkage are synthetic design assumptions, not certified capacity or actual engine equipment.

The arm-only probes admit the same original worker body5 at low and raised external-handle positions. Those probes do not include a tool and do not prove contacts, path clearance, acquisition, carry or deposit. All remain child.2/.3 gates. Four carton walls, the source unit IDs, worker body geometry and existing joint limits remain intact.

Prompt files and built-in tool provenance are recorded under `assets/references/v3/{prompts,provenance}`. Root acceptance covers reference/metric plan only. One initially held tool is a prepared visual state; full warehouse integration must account for its acquisition/stow separately.
