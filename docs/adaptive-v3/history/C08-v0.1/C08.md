# C08 — loaded motion and handling design

The frozen [contract](../../v3/contract-proposals/vehicle-handling-v3.json) binds six forklift HU types to the accepted L05/L07/L09/L12 geometry. This is synthetic analytical design. Actual mesh/contact/lock/transfer and source scheduling acceptance remain M/P/W gates; `operational_admission` stays false. No real equipment certification, inventory or operational KPI is inferred.

All eight C02 templates remain unchanged. SMALL/STANDARD retain manual/cart/light-belt handling and do not acquire forklift permission. Each forklift HU keeps its exact width, height, depth, gross mass and upright local XYZ. Relative load yaw/tilt or scale changes reject before a route cost exists. Whole loaded vehicle yaw is permitted only on the accepted analytic primitive; reverse motion preserves physical chassis yaw. Source 5-degree witnesses are not chord interpolation instructions.

All fork vertices stay inside the unchanged L05 vehicle box; continuous route proofs remain conservative. Bare and direct-HU fork top stays .30. L05/R12 historical sources are preserved, not silently edited.

Actual M14 attachment mesh must expose the two through-cavities, load-bearing pad surfaces and visible locks. A boolean analytical witness cannot substitute for that acceptance.

Direct-HU forks require two actual base contacts and a clear under-HU insertion path. A solid HU resting directly on the ground or on a continuous solid deck has no such path and cannot be animated as a pickup. M03 fixed low supports may use three supported deck ribs with open top entry lanes while retaining exact outer/usable/support bounds. Stored HU bottom stays at its C04 anchor; an extra carrier underneath cannot silently raise it. Temporary port/buffer supports must be represented and cleared too. M04/M14 must jointly plan LONG rack-arm, pad and pocket relief before geometry: a continuous solid cross-deck through the attachment would block withdrawal. These are physical model requirements, not already passed transfer checks. The188 reserve/height-blocked slots remain unavailable.

Each load component is enclosed by an accepted component, not just the overall union AABB. Non-LONG loads fit the existing bare-vehicle bounding component, but still use `loaded_max` on main edges; the bare-only policy is retained. Local profiles are unchanged `nonlong_forklift` and `long_loaded`. For every one of1,316 compiled directed arcs, the contract intersects cargo permissions, geometry profile and height. Original analytic yaw/ICR/root offset and reservation groups remain authoritative.
