## Planning and storage follow-up

The backend adds a separate authenticated planning chain with explicit caller
choices. History contains56 complete days for the pinned200 source SKUs, declared
synthetic provenance and a fixed eight-hour local/UTC window before the test day.
Mean28 and HGB share a42-day training and14-day rolling-validation policy.
Validation actual counts use exact integer sums. CPU observations remain separate
from the semantic plan identity.

The projected finite outbound offers retain the full source SKU universe and
caller-selected, source-permitted port shares. Stock binding retains every offer,
shortage and original HU, using each HU at most once.
The placement decision includes source compatibility, relocation/return cost and
the declared customer cost. A nonpositive candidate retains a no-op result.

`python -m app.cli v3 plan-offers` freezes the four raw caller inputs before
calculation and publishes history, forecast, projection, stock and decision bytes.
`python -m app.cli v3 validate-offer-plan --bundle DIR` authenticates the complete
saved chain through its authoritative loaders. A failed attempt preserves its
completed stages. Full command examples and explicit JSON choices are in the
[planning guide](proposals/adaptive-v3/offer-planning-cli.md).

The registered `GET /api/v3/coordinator-runs/{run_id}/storage-fullness` projection
reports occupied/empty/reserved/blocked slots, active capacity, stored units and
exact mass/volume at the horizon and end. It reads saved native artifacts. The
ordinary saved source observation has530 occupied physical slots out of776,
530 active occupied slots out of588,12,250 stored units and9,403.64kg of slot mass.
Overall onsite mass also includes retained carrier materials and has a separate
scope. A reader/API test and a source-data observation are distinct evidence.

The external captured-state viewer proposal reads actual manifest checkpoints.
Its protocol, raw-byte, DOM and build checks have passed. Browser visual review
remains pending because local browser use is disabled in Work settings. The
proposal has not been applied to the frontend. Captured-state navigation and
arbitrary-prefix replay retain separate performance requirements.
